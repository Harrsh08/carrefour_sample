Brand Marketing Message

Dove Lavender Shampoo (400ml) and Conditioner (320ml) is a professionally engineered hair care system designed to provide intensive volume and structural restoration for thin or flat hair. This high-performance duo utilizes the calming and revitalizing properties of Lavender oil and Rosemary extract—botanicals biologically selected for their ability to soothe the scalp and invigorate the hair fiber. The shampoo acts by gently removing environmental impurities and excess sebum, while the conditioner works to detangle and smooth the cuticle without adding heavy lipid weight. This synergistic process ensures that hair remains fundamentally buoyant, resilient, and full of natural body.The advanced formula is part of the Dove Nourishing Secrets range, inspired by traditional French beauty rituals to deliver a salon-quality sensory experience. The shampoo is engineered to provide a rich, aromatic lather that rinses clean, while the weightless conditioner targets the hair’s mid-lengths and ends to enhance shine and manageability. Together, they work to improve the hair’s visible thickness and elasticity, leaving every strand feeling fundamentally softer and looking flawlessly polished. Free from harsh chemical buildup, this set provides a balanced approach to hair health that prioritizes fiber vitality and a pristine, voluminous appearance.
